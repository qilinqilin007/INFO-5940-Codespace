Through this assignment, my biggest takeaway was a clearer understanding of the importance of prompts and a systematic learning of prompt writing tips. The biggest challenge in this learning process was how to effectively write appropriate prompts.

Initially, I simply gave it instructions in conversational language, but the feedback wasn't as expected. The AI ​​agent didn't seem to understand my meaning. Its output was always too brief or lacked key points, seemingly only understanding a part of my meaning. I tried modifying it based on its feedback, which led to my prompts becoming longer and longer, but the output became increasingly strange. The AI ​​agent didn't seem to be able to capture my complete thoughts well.

At this point, I realized that perhaps my prompts lacked logic and framework. To improve this, I researched prompt writing tips. I started breaking down the prompts into several modules, writing my instructions according to the formula of role, scene, action, constraint, and example. The goal was to provide the AI ​​with a background framework before it responded, clarifying the intent and allowing it to produce a more effective and expected response. Finally, I uploaded an ideal output style as a reference, allowing it to generate the final plan according to that style. The quality of the travel plans generated by ai-agent was significantly improved.

During this process, I gained a more concrete understanding of the concepts discussed in class. I realized that the significance of prompts lies in optimizing the model's output by adjusting the input structure and content, resulting in responses that better meet expectations. My innovation involved anthropomorphizing the agent. I gave them real-life professions and identities, treating them and giving instructions as real people. I believe these prompts help the agent better empathize with the situation and understand the desired outcome.

Throughout the process, I used ChatGPT to help refine my prompts. After writing the prompts by myself and running the agent, I found its output wasn't very good. It only provided the initial plan and points for modification, but not the final, revised plan. This made it difficult to read. However, modifying the prompts didn't significantly improve the situation. So, I described the problem to ChatGPT, and they suggested using bulletpoints to give prompt. After trying this, I saw the expected results. I suspect this is because bulletpoints are shorter, resulting in lower input load for AI-agent input and better retention of context.
